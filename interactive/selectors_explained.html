How each asset selector chooses portfolio candidates

The selectors run after data preprocessing and Ledoit-Wolf covariance estimation. Their job is to reduce the asset universe into a candidate list before the weight optimizer, such as PSO, ACO, GWO, EBGWO, APSO, CLPSO, or LAPSO, solves the allocation problem.
